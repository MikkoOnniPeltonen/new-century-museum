# Portrait and notable-work image review

Reviewed 16–17 September 2026. Scope: the 20 portraits and 20 work/context images
mapped by `scripts/optimize-images.mjs` and displayed from `public/images/`.
Originals are in `assets-src/images/portraits/` and `assets-src/images/notableWorks/`.
Each row below covers one displayed image; IDs identify the corresponding WebP files.
Unused originals (including the additional Mandela and Bolívar images) are outside this inventory.

## Conclusion

**The image collection is not yet fully authenticated.** There are definite image/title
mismatches, an unsuitable attribution for Amo's portrait, later representations that need
dated captions, and images whose artist, photographer, date or source remains unresolved.
These findings do not establish deliberate tampering. Visual inspection also cannot certify
that every file is unaltered or determine AI origin reliably.

This is an editorial provenance review, not physical-artwork authentication. Evidence includes
visible title pages, embedded metadata, catalog records and composition comparisons. A likely
catalog candidate is explicitly distinguished from a verified source. A historical artwork
can be authentic as an artwork without being a contemporary or accurate likeness.

The initial audit made no exhibit changes. The approved follow-up on 17 September
withholds 2 portraits (Amo and Wang) and 7 work images (Amo, Bahá'u'lláh, Bolívar,
Wang, Müteferrika, Lovelace and Liu), substituting explicitly labelled abstract
collection markers. Originals remain preserved on disk. No historical facsimile
replacements have yet been sourced. Per-image notes and cautious captions are now
shown in rooms and person drawers; unresolved imagery is not labelled authenticated.
The inventory below preserves the assessment of the original assets.

## Priority actions

1. **Amo portrait:** do not present this as an authenticated likeness of Amo. The painting
   matches the composition circulated as *Gannibal*, whose own sitter attribution/provenance
   is uncertain. This does not establish the actual sitter. Use a clearly labelled placeholder
   unless a defensible portrait source is found. [Catalog comparison][gannibal];
   [scholarly discussion of false Amo portraits][amo-portraits].
2. **Bahá'u'lláh work:** the visible title page names *The Revelation of Bahá'u'lláh*,
   **by Horace Holley**. It is not the *Kitáb-i-Aqdas*, nor a book authored by Bahá'u'lláh.
   Replace it with a sourced edition of the featured work. [Scanned book][holley].
3. **Amo work:** the image is the title page of his **1734 dissertation**, not the
   **1738 treatise** named in the record. Change either the selected work or the image,
   retaining a bibliographic source. [Berlin digitized dissertation][amo-book].
4. **Bolívar work:** a later historical painting is shown where the *Jamaica Letter*
   is featured. It is not a reproduction of the letter. A candidate is Daniel Hernández's
   *Capitulación de Ayacucho* (1924), but that exact attribution remains provisional here.
   Prefer the actual document. [Document record][jamaica]; [painting candidate][ayacucho].
5. **Wang Zhenyi:** neither illustration has an established source identifying the sitter
   or connecting it to her eclipse text. Do not label either as an authenticated portrait
   or an image of her original work without additional evidence.
6. **Nzinga and Mandela:** the portrait of Nzinga is an **1830s later depiction**;
   the Mandela prison-window image corresponds to a **1994 return visit**, not a photograph
   taken during imprisonment. Make the distinction visible. [NPG][nzinga]; [archive][mandela].

## Rights and integrity

- **Authenticity and permission are separate.** This review grants no copyright clearance.
- The Nzinga original carries National Portrait Gallery copyright metadata. Modern photos
  and the Mandela revisit photograph also require image-specific reuse checks and credits.
  Museum/archive hosting alone does not mean public domain.
- Preserve artist/photographer, object date, depicted-event date, institution, catalog URL,
  license/permission, and crop/processing notes for every retained asset.
- The inspected optimization script rotates/resizes originals and encodes WebP; it does not
  synthesize historical imagery. That does not authenticate the upstream original files.
- Some originals are only thumbnail-sized. Enlarging them does not restore documentary detail;
  replacements should come from reputable high-resolution sources with documented reuse terms.
- Public captions should distinguish **contemporary portrait**, **later depiction**,
  **document facsimile**, and **contextual illustration**. If evidence is absent, say so or
  use a neutral placeholder rather than an invented likeness.
